# LLM Family Comparison

| Model family | Owner | Open or closed weights | Context window | Main strengths |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GPT | OpenAI | Mostly closed; OpenAI also offers open-weight models | Depends on the specific model | General assistance, reasoning, coding, tool use |
| Claude | Anthropic | Closed weights | Depends on the specific model; some support long contexts | Writing, coding, document analysis |
| Llama | Meta | Open-weight models with model-specific license terms | Depends on the specific model; some support long contexts | Self-hosting, customization, local deployment |
| Gemini | Google | Closed weights for its hosted models | Depends on the specific model; some support long contexts | Multimodal tasks, reasoning, long-context processing |

## Important Notes

- Context-window sizes change by model and version. Always identify the exact model before quoting a token limit.
- Open weights do not automatically mean unrestricted commercial use; review the relevant license.
- Hosted API pricing varies by model and input/output token rates.
- Model strengths are general descriptions, not guarantees of performance on every task.
